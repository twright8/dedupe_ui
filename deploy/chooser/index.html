TI UK data tools

Three tools for matching names in company and political finance data. Pick the one you need.

  • ROE / OCOD

    ROE–OCOD linkage

    Links owners in the Register of Overseas Entities to the titles they hold in the Land Registry overseas ownership data.

    Open→
  • Donations

    Donations reconciliation

    Finds the records in the Electoral Commission donations data that belong to the same donor, and gives each donor one identifier.

    Open→
  • PSC

    PSC reconciliation

    Finds the people with significant control at Companies House who are the same person or business, and gives each one identifier.

    Open→
One login covers all three tools.
